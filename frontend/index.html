CORE RESEARCH CAPABILITIES

Evidence shapes the next scan

Scheduling combines learned structure with safeguards against stale or incomplete knowledge.

01 / INFERENCE

Conditional non-detection

A MISS is not automatically absence. Temporal overlap determines how informative the observation is.

LOW OVERLAP→WEAK EVIDENCE
02 / LEARNING

Online transition learning

Scan history reveals cross-band sequences as emitter activity moves through the spectrum.

B2→B6→B9
03 / SCHEDULING

Confidence-guided scheduling

Transition support and evidence confidence help prioritize useful candidate scans.

SUPPORT + EVIDENCE→PRIORITY
04 / COVERAGE

Discovery protection

Discovery priority gives neglected bands a chance to challenge familiar patterns.

UNOBSERVED BAND→PRIORITY RISES
05 / MEMORY

Evidence decay

Old confidence fades over time so yesterday’s pattern does not dominate today’s decisions.

STALE EVIDENCE→DECAY
06 / RECOVERY

Behaviour-change recovery

Detect a changed pattern, explore uncertain bands, and update the transition model.
